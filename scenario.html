Removing cars doesn't just change transportation. It affects where people live, how cities grow, how goods move, how we work, and what our streets become.

FIRST-ORDER EFFECTS 02 / 05
01

Transportation

Public transport becomes the backbone of everyday travel. Walking and cycling become much more common.

02

03
